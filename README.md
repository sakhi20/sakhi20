## 🔬 Featured Research

### [Disaster Damage Detection via Satellite Imagery](https://github.com/sakhi20/Disaster-Damage-Detection)
> Two-phase deep learning pipeline (building localization + damage classification)
> on the xBD benchmark. **JointDamageNet achieves F1=0.8136 with a single model**,
> surpassing the xView2 Challenge 42-model ensemble winner (F1=0.8112) and
> achieving the highest localization F1 (0.8752) of all compared methods.
> Benchmarked 7 geospatial foundation models; fine-tuned DeepLabV3+ with a
> ResNet-50 backbone to Dice 0.877 / IoU 0.781, outperforming Satlas and DINOv2.
